World: five island regions, 23 ports, scattered uninhabited cays, unrestricted open-ocean sailing.
Narrative content: 1 introduction, 40 main-story missions, 10 side adventures.
Ordinary work: multiple repeatable harbour-board jobs can be carried together.
Saving: multiple named .tide files; each save remembers the last port visited and loads the flagship there.

V17 update: ambient ships and pirates continue moving during harbour interactions; each port has an automatic coastal gun battery.

V18 update: merchantmen, pirates and naval patrols now fight independently in the living sea; NPC battle state is stored in .tide saves.

Version 20 additions: commander reputation/notoriety records are maintained in tidebound/mixins/traffic.py, displayed by the N-key ledger in tidebound/mixins/rendering.py, and persisted through tidebound/mixins/save_load.py.
